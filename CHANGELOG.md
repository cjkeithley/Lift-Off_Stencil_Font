# Changelog

Lift-Off follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

- **Patch** (1.0.x): outline fixes that don't change spacing.
- **Minor** (1.x.0): new glyphs or OpenType features.
- **Major** (x.0.0): changes that move text in existing documents (metrics, spacing).

Font files carry the version as `Version M.mmp; M.m.p` (e.g. 1.2.3 is "Version 1.023; 1.2.3").

## [1.0.0] - unreleased

First public release.

- Two styles in one family: **Lift-Off Regular** (clean outlines) and **Lift-Off Worn** (paint bleed and wear, no sharp corners).
- Capitals A–Z, digits 0–9 and the full ASCII punctuation set. Lowercase keys type the capitals.
- Monospaced cell (894 units) based on the stencilling on the underside of a Block II DSKY digital indicator (SER 340A), confirmed against calibrated photos: 0.142" pitch, 0.112" cap height at 11.5 pt.
- Period and comma sit tight against the preceding letter. A period followed by a space closes up, as in "REV. C" on the unit; stylistic set 1 (ss01, "Spaced period") keeps a normal space.
